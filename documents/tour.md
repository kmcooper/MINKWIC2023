# Accessibility Checklist  
Which of these do you see? Check the box and take notes.

---

## Entrances & Physical Access
- [ ] Step-free entrances (ramps or level entry)  
- [ ] Automatic or easy-to-open doors  
- [ ] Doors wide enough for mobility devices  
- [ ] Clear, unobstructed pathways  
- [ ] Accessible parking nearby  

---

## Navigation
- [ ] Clear, consistent signage throughout  
- [ ] Logical, intuitive layout  
- [ ] Maps or directories at key points  
- [ ] Braille and tactile signage  
- [ ] Color and symbol cues (not just text)  

---

## Multi-Sensory Navigation
- [ ] Elevators with audible floor announcements  
- [ ] Audible signals for transitions (doors, alerts)  
- [ ] Visual + auditory cues (not just one)  
- [ ] Tactile indicators (textured flooring, etc.)  
- [ ] Visible and audible alarms  

---

## Visual Accessibility
- [ ] High contrast signage and text  
- [ ] Large, readable fonts  
- [ ] Good lighting (not too dim or glaring)  
- [ ] Minimal visual clutter  
- [ ] No reliance on color alone  

---

## Tactile & Physical Interaction
- [ ] Buttons are reachable  
- [ ] Buttons clearly labeled  
- [ ] Buttons include tactile/Braille where appropriate  
- [ ] Controls usable from seated or standing positions  
- [ ] Surfaces provide physical feedback  
- [ ] Accessible bathrooms with grab bars  
- [ ] Light switches at reachable height  

---

## Classroom & Learning Spaces
- [ ] Flexible seating (wheelchairs, different body types)  
- [ ] Clear sightlines to screens/boards  
- [ ] Microphones or amplification available  
- [ ] Adjustable podiums or teaching stations  
- [ ] Materials readable from multiple positions  

---

## Acoustic Environment
- [ ] Minimal background noise  
- [ ] Limited echo (good acoustics)  
- [ ] Quiet spaces available nearby  
- [ ] Sound systems support clear speech  

---

## Facilities
- [ ] Accessible restrooms nearby  
- [ ] Clear signage to restrooms  
- [ ] Fixtures at usable heights  
- [ ] Private or gender-inclusive options  
- [ ] Simple, consistent layout  
- [ ] Clear labeling of rooms  
- [ ] Instructions easy to understand  
- [ ] No unnecessary complexity  

---

## Technology & Instructional Access
- [ ] Screens readable from all seating areas  
- [ ] Captioning available for video/audio  
- [ ] Equipment usable without fine motor precision  
- [ ] Multiple ways to access materials
